# Post-mortem INC-2026-05: payment provider rate limit

Date: 2026-06-03. Facilitator: Priya. Blameless.

## Timeline
- 05-29 23:02 month-end batch starts, 1 840 tenants.
- 23:11 provider returns intermittent 503 (their incident, confirmed later).
- 23:11 to 23:13 our retries: five attempts, 500 ms base, per charge. About
  210 retries in ninety seconds.
- 23:13 provider returns 429 for every call. Account locked for eleven
  minutes.
- 23:24 lock lifts; remaining batch completes on the 00:30 re-run.

## Findings
- F-027: retry burst tripped the provider's per-account limiter.
- The provider integration guide (2024) states three attempts, two second
  base delay, honour Retry-After. Nobody on the team had read it.
- The re-run was safe only because the failed charges had never reached
  the provider. Had they succeeded and timed out, we would have repeated
  INC-2025-02 (the idempotency header is still not implemented).

## Actions
- ADR-012 written (Mara). Supersedes ADR-009 for payment calls.
- Idempotency header moved to the top of the payment backlog, again.
- Add "payment retries" to the review checklist.
